Neuro-Acoustic Reader

idle

The Neuro-Acoustic Reader plays an audiobook chapter aloud while reading your EEG brainwave data in the background. It watches the ratio between your alpha and beta band power — a rough proxy for "zoned out" versus "paying attention" — and the instant that ratio crosses a threshold, it pauses the narration, generates a one-sentence recap of what you just missed, speaks it, and picks the story back up right where you left off. No account, no install — upload a recording or try the bundled sample below.
